## Instructions to run the prototype website:


1) Open site. You will see the default page. There is a pre-filled row. If you wish, you can
use the arrows to go through the different movies on this row.

2) To open the sidebar, click the top left purple button. On the sidebar, select the icon
labeled "row library."

3) This will open a side column which allows you to select either pre-made rows or saved
rows. Click on the add icon next to the preset row, Mysteries. This will add a new row of
mystery films and shows to your home page.

4) On the side bar, select the icon labeled 'customize filters.'

5) This will open two side columns. On the central column, there is a list of potential criteria
for your new row. Select the criteria
(A) mystery
(B) comedy
(C) upbeat

*(Note: due to the limits of our implementation, you can only add these criteria specifically, and in
this order.)*

6) Then select "Add Row" on the bottom of the selector screen. This will add a final third row
to the home page. To exit out of the sidebar view, click on the X button on the top right of
the sidebar. Scroll to the bottom of the home page and in the new row, find the movie
*Clue* and select it.

7) This will open a modal page for the movie *Clue*. On the left side, there’s the rating and
movie summary. On the right-hand side, there is a box informing you of the streaming
services that *Clue* is available on. Click on the Microsoft button and it will lead you to the
Microsoft store page for *Clue*. To exit out of the modal, click on the X button on the top
right corner.
